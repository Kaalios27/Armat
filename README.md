# Armat

A personal study system for European Spanish (Castilian, with vosotros). One focused hour a day: vocabulary, conjugation and grammar, Monday to Saturday, and a 60-minute examination on Sunday. The system plans what to study, schedules revision with FSRS-7, tracks every answer, and adapts to weaknesses and exam results. The interface and all explanations are available in English (default) or French.

**Open it: https://kaalios27.github.io/Armat/**

Armat runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no account and no API key: the app, its database and your progress all live on your device. The study content (lessons, exercises, example sentences, dictation texts, model answers) was written with AI assistance in advance and ships with the site, so no AI service is called while you study.

Architecture and design decisions: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## What it does

- **Daily session**: three 20-minute modules in fixed order, with timing, pause and resume, and autosave. The timer pauses after three minutes without input.
  - Vocabulary: about 465 curated items (false friends, discourse markers, idioms, academic and professional vocabulary), recognition and typed, accent-sensitive production, cloze in context, and FSRS-7 scheduling with learning steps.
  - Conjugation: 29 units from the present to the pluperfect subjunctive, with a deterministic conjugator (about 120 verbs, all tenses, vosotros, reflexive imperatives with correct accents). Errors are classified as wrong person, wrong tense, ending, irregular verb, missing accent, stem change or missing pronoun.
  - Grammar: 56 concepts in nine chapters, with curated lessons and exercises for the first 14.
- **Sunday exam**: dictation (15 points), conjugation (20), grammar (20), translation (15), writing (30), built from the week's history, failed items and weaknesses. Every fourth exam is cumulative (40% last four weeks, 40% older material, 20% weaknesses).
  - Dictation, conjugation and closed grammar items are graded exactly; open answers get an approximate score from the closest reference answer.
  - The writing is self-assessed: after submitting, you compare your text with a model answer and score five criteria from 0 to 6.
- **Learner model**: concept mastery (weighted accuracy), FSRS revision of concepts, decaying weaknesses, an adaptive new-word pace, and a level estimate from demonstrated competence.

## Your data

- Progress is saved in this browser (IndexedDB), on this device only. Your PC and your iPad each keep their own progress.
- **Settings > Your data**: export your progress to a file, import it on another device, or erase it. Export regularly: it is your backup.
- On iPad and iPhone, add Armat to the Home Screen (Share > Add to Home Screen). Safari can otherwise delete the data of a website that has not been opened for several weeks.
- Armat opens in one tab at a time, because two tabs writing the same database would overwrite each other.
- The dictation is read by your device's Spanish voices. On iPad, more voices can be added in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices; choose one in Armat's settings.

## How far the curated content goes

| Area | Curated | Lasts about |
|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary | ~465 words with examples | 4 to 6 weeks (18 new words per session at the standard pace) |
| Grammar | 14 of 56 lessons, with exercises | 3 to 5 weeks (one new concept at most per day, gated by mastery) |
| Conjugation | 29 units, drills generated by the conjugator | several months (mastery needs at least two sessions per unit) |
| Exams | 24 dictation texts and 18 writing prompts with model answers, by level | the whole programme |

When the written grammar lessons run out, the grammar module keeps reviewing the concepts already introduced; when the vocabulary runs out, sessions continue with reviews. More lessons and words are added by extending the files in `src/content/`.

## Run locally

Requirements: Node.js 24 or later.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:3000
npm test           # Vitest: conjugator, FSRS, scheduling, sessions, exams, export and import (PGlite)
npm run typecheck
npm run build      # static site in out/
```

Optional development settings (simulated date, shorter modules) are described in `.env.example`.

## Publishing

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs the type checker, the tests and the build on every push and pull request. On `main`, it publishes the static site to GitHub Pages (repository Settings > Pages > Source: GitHub Actions).

## Notes

- The exam timer counts while the exam page is open; answers autosave to the database and to the browser's storage. The exam is not cheat-proof, by design: the dictation text and answer keys are on your device.
- Nothing is sent anywhere: the site makes no network requests after loading, apart from loading its own files.
